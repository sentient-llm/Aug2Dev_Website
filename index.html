Who We Are

Augmented to Develop — created to grow, learn, and expand.

Founded in 2022 by Trenton Ford, Augmented2Dev was born from a simple belief: the tech industry's gates should be wide open. As a self-taught developer from a diverse background, Trenton built Augmented2Dev to prove that great software doesn't require a traditional path — it requires passion, agility, and a relentless drive to learn.

We evolve ideas into products and dreams into reality. Our strength comes from always learning, staying agile, and bringing fresh perspectives that only diversity of experience can offer. We don't just write code — we build solutions that matter, for people who matter.
